Hi there, I'm Kerri. I'm a former web developer turned art student and lifelong gamer, welcome to my blog. Here you will find various opinions, reviews and thoughts that I've chosen to share with the world. Please make yourself at home. I play many games &mdash; TTRPGs, board, video, and card &mdash; as well as enjoy my time sharing the work of other artists through twitter. 

Outside of creative amd playful endeavours I work to support my local LGBTQ+ community as a Youth Worker and Transender Rights Activist. I've helped facilitate a nationwide Pride Festival two years in a row and have helped to impact national policy decisions on protecting the rights of the gender diverse.

Returning to college at 27 to pursue an Art Foundation Course has been an exciting adventure and I can't wait to share the skills I learn along the way.
